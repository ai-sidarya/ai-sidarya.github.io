EPS Engineering Leadership Academy

A working guide for a Lead Software Engineering Manager interview in Electronic Power Steering. It teaches enough to ask sharp questions, make defensible decisions and speak credibly to engineers, safety, security and customers. It does not try to turn you into the specialists you will lead.

How to study

Chapters marked with a star in the sidebar are core: the roughly 20% that decides most interviews. Others are depth for follow-ups. Every chapter has the same shape: what you must know, how it works, an EPS example, how it fails, what a manager decides, what the interviewer tests, weak versus strong answers, a self-check, and an exercise. Reading alone does not prepare you; say the answers aloud and time them.

Standards and source policy

The Sources page logs what was checked and when. Anything not logged is practitioner judgment and is marked so. Standards text is summarized, never reproduced.
