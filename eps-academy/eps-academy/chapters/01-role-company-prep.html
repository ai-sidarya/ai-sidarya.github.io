Chapter 01: Role and Bosch preparation

Source statusWritten from practitioner knowledge. Not yet verified line by line against primary sources; confirm standards details before quoting them to a panel.

1. Decode the posting before anything else Core

The posting tells you what the panel will probe. Highlight every noun phrase and sort it into four buckets: delivery (customer milestones, release, escalation), technical (safety, security, embedded, AUTOSAR, CI/CD), people (global teams, matrix) and change (AI, SAFe, lean). Count each bucket. The heaviest bucket is where your first and hardest questions will land. If the posting is customer-heavy, expect escalation scenarios before architecture questions.

2. Understand your reporting line Level 2

If your manager is a portfolio manager for the software stack, your work sits between a shared platform and several customer projects. The recurring tension is platform reuse versus customer-specific scope. Prepare one story where you held that line, and one question for the panel on how platform-versus-project priority is decided today.

3. Research that is worth the time Level 2

  • Bosch's public pages on Vehicle Motion and steering systems: product families, OEM-facing claims, press releases.
  • Bosch's public descriptions of their software and safety culture, only what is published.
  • The posting's location and team name, then ask what its program mix is.
Do not inventYou do not know their internal tools, architecture, org or customers. Say "I would expect" or "in my experience at similar suppliers." A confident wrong claim about their internals is the fastest way to lose credibility.

4. Close the credibility gap this week Level 3

Do three concrete things so your answers rest on real experience: (1) ask an engineer to walk you through one real release pipeline run and note where evidence is collected; (2) read one real software safety plan or release checklist; (3) try GitHub Copilot on a small, non-confidential task and record what it got wrong. Each gives you one honest sentence for the interview.

6. Questions to ask them Level 2

  • What does a late release usually trace back to here: scope, dependencies, evidence or integration?
  • How are platform and customer-project priorities decided?
  • How is AI-assisted development governed today?
  • What would success look like in 90 days?

7. Self-check and exercise

Exercise: Paste the posting into a document and highlight it in four colors. Write one sentence of proof from your career for each bucket.
