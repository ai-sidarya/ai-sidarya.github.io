Chapter 14: Customer escalation leadership

Source statusPractitioner knowledge. Not yet checked line by line against primary sources. Verify before quoting to a panel.
TakeawayCustomers forgive bad news. They do not forgive surprises. Facts first, plan second, dates as ranges.

1. What an escalation is Core

An escalation is a customer saying our normal process failed. Triggers: missed date, field issue, repeated defect, safety concern, or lost confidence. The technical problem is half of it. The other half is trust.

2. The first 24 hours Level 2

Contain→Establish facts→One owner→First update→Recovery plan
  • Contain: stop the damage. Hold affected releases, protect vehicles in test.
  • Facts: what is known, what is not, what is assumed. Label each.
  • One owner and one source of truth. Split ownership creates conflicting messages.
  • Cadence: agree update times and keep them, even when the update is "no change."

4. Uncertainty, ranges and saying no Level 3

Give a range with the assumptions behind it. Narrow it as evidence arrives. When the request is unreasonable, say no and offer options: less scope, a later date, more resources, or a staged release. Explain what each option costs.

Do not speculateNever name a root cause you have not confirmed. Never promise a date you cannot defend. Never promise to waive evidence.

5. Cause, corrective action, prevention Level 2

Root cause explains why it happened. Escape point is where we should have caught it. Corrective action fixes this instance. Prevention changes the system so it cannot recur. Customers want all four.

6. Avoiding escalation fatigue

War rooms are for the first days. After that, shrink the group, keep the cadence, and move to normal tracking. Daily calls with everyone burn out the team and add no information.

7. Self-check and exercise

Exercise: A customer reports unintended assist on a test vehicle running your latest build, four weeks before a gate. Notes: contain (stop test vehicles on that build), involve the safety manager at once, capture logs and configuration, name one owner, send a factual update within hours, then publish a recovery plan with a range, not a promise.
