5. Case Study E — Pipeline Green but Vehicle Validation Fails Core

Facts

All CI pipelines are green: SIL, HIL, static analysis, coding guidelines. Vehicle validation shows steering oscillations in a narrow maneuver. OEM is confused: “How can everything be green but the car fails?”

Unknowns

  • Is the maneuver missing from regression tests?
  • Is the pipeline masking flaky tests?
  • Is the integration environment different from vehicle environment?
  • Is calibration misaligned with software version?

Options

  • Option A: Declare pipeline unreliable and rebuild it (too slow).
  • Option B: Identify pipeline blind spots and patch coverage.
  • Option C: Freeze release and run full cross‑environment comparison.

Recommended Decision

Choose Option B: patch pipeline blind spots while running targeted vehicle tests. Avoid full rebuild unless systemic issues appear.

First 24 Hours

  • Reproduce maneuver in SIL/HIL; confirm missing coverage.
  • Check calibration–software alignment.
  • Audit pipeline for skipped or flaky tests.
  • Prepare OEM briefing: root cause hypothesis, coverage gap, fix plan.

Communication

To OEM: “Pipeline is green but coverage gap exists for this maneuver. We are adding targeted tests and validating fixes. Vehicle behavior will be re‑tested within 72 hours.”

Prevention

  • Add vehicle‑derived maneuvers to regression suite.
  • Introduce pipeline coverage dashboards.
  • Track calibration–software alignment automatically.

Tough Follow‑Up

Ask test and pipeline leads: “Why did pipeline coverage diverge from vehicle reality? What structural guardrail prevents this again?”

6. Case Study F — Platform Versus Customer Scope Core

Facts

EPS software is built on a shared platform used by multiple OEMs. One OEM requests a custom steering feel mode requiring platform changes. Platform team says “out of scope.” Customer team says “must deliver.” Conflict escalates.

Unknowns

  • Is the requested feature truly platform‑level?
  • Can it be implemented as OEM‑specific configuration?
  • Does platform roadmap allow such changes?
  • What does contract say about platform flexibility?

Options

  • Option A: Force platform change (risks other OEMs).
  • Option B: Implement OEM‑specific extension layer.
  • Option C: Reject feature (damages relationship).

Recommended Decision

Choose Option B: OEM‑specific extension layer unless platform change is clearly justified and safe.

First 24 Hours

  • Review interface agreements and platform scope.
  • Identify whether feature touches safety‑critical logic.
  • Draft extension‑layer proposal with constraints.
  • Align with platform and customer teams.

Communication

To OEM: “Feature can be delivered via OEM‑specific extension without impacting other customers. Platform remains stable. Here is the timeline and safety analysis.”

Prevention

  • Define platform versus OEM scope explicitly.
  • Introduce extension‑layer architecture for customization.
  • Align platform roadmap with customer expectations.

Tough Follow‑Up

Ask platform lead: “Where is our boundary unclear? How do we prevent future scope conflicts?”
